Year One
Building Foundations
Semester 1
Musical Identity HL/SL
Tue Oct 27th
  • Presenting Music — Solo performance
  • Music Analysis — Elements of Music
  • Research Project — personal musical background
  • Reflection on musical influences & identity
This opening unit anchors the whole course in each student's own musical voice before formal assessment language enters the picture. Students perform a solo piece for the class — a first, low-stakes run at the performer role that Presenting Music will eventually assess — while starting a research project that traces the musical influences and formative experiences that shaped them. The Elements of Music work here builds the shared vocabulary (rhythm, timbre, texture, form) every later unit assumes students already have.

The Baroque Era HL/SL
Fri Nov 5th / Wed Nov 25th
  • Baroque Era Portfolio (BEP) — analysis & performance
  • Jazz, Strings & Swing Exploration Portfolio (SSEP)
  • Historical context & stylistic conventions
Two parallel portfolios anchor this unit: the Baroque Era Portfolio, pairing stylistic analysis with a short performance that demonstrates Baroque conventions — ornamentation, terraced dynamics, a basso continuo texture — and the Jazz, Strings & Swing Exploration Portfolio, which introduces a contrasting 20th-century idiom for comparison. Together they give students their first real practice connecting a work's historical context to specific creating and performing conventions, the exact move Exploring Music in Context will later ask of them under assessment conditions.

Exploring Music in Context (EMC) S1 HL/SL
Wed Dec 10th
  • EMC Portfolio Draft
Students submit a full draft of Exploring Music in Context — the first externally-assessed component they'll complete, worth 40% at SL and 30% at HL. The draft is deliberately due mid-semester so there's real runway for feedback and revision before the Semester 2 final submission: treat it as a rehearsal for the actual deadline, not a formality.

Year Two
Completing All Components
Semester 1
Our Local Context HL/SL
Thu Sept 3rd
  • Experimenting with Music — Simulations
The first unit of Experimenting with Music, built around simulated creating and performing tasks grounded in music from Ho Chi Minh City and the wider region. Students practise responding quickly to unfamiliar stimulus material under realistic time constraints — the same format the final component uses — while grounding the work in a musical context close to home.

CMM Draft Submissions
HL OnlyWed Oct 28th
  • CMM Product (nearing completion)
  • Project Plan
  • Draft Budget
HL students bring their Contemporary Music Maker product to near-final shape and submit supporting planning documents — a project plan mapping remaining tasks and timeline, and a draft budget accounting for the resources their concept requires. CMM is the HL-only fourth component, assessed independently of the three SL components, so this checkpoint exists to catch scope or feasibility problems while there's still time to adjust.

Experimenting with Music HL/SL
Mon Dec 14th
  • Presenting Music — Latin Music Compositions
  • Music Analysis — Elements of Music
  • Research Project
Students complete the main body of Experimenting with Music — the course's process-focused component — while composing original works in Latin styles that can also become candidates for the Presenting Music portfolio. The research strand asks students to document how their experiments evolved, since this component assesses the creative process itself, not just the finished result.

Semester 2
Head of Arts
HL OnlyFri Jan 15th / Fri Jan 22nd
  • Share Slides & Project Conference — Feedback
  • Multimedia Presentations — Final Major Task
The project wraps up in two stages a week apart. On Jan 15th, students share slides and take part in a project conference for structured feedback on where things stand. The following week, on Jan 22nd, they deliver a multimedia presentation as the unit's final major task — the version of the Head of Arts brief that ultimately feeds into their Contemporary Music Maker concept.

Exploring Music in Context (EMC) S2 HL/SL
Fri Mar 19th
  • EMC Uploads 1, 2 & 3 (Finale)
  • Finalise written commentary & musical examples
  • Submit to IBIS — component complete
This is the real deadline: the December draft becomes a finished, IBIS-ready submission. Students finalize the written commentary alongside the musical examples that support it, checking that every claim in the writing is actually evidenced in the audio or score excerpts attached. Once uploaded, Exploring Music in Context is complete and locked — there's no revising it after the IBIS window closes.

Latin Music Themed Recital HL/SL
Wed May 6th
  • Presenting Music — Live Performance
  • Latin rhythms, styles & cultural context
  • Programme notes & reflection
A public performance built around Latin music traditions — clave-based rhythms, montuno textures, a stylistic range from bossa nova to salsa — that doubles as authentic practice for the live-performance side of Presenting Music. Students write programme notes introducing their chosen pieces, the same skill the final Presenting Music submission requires, and reflect afterward on what worked and what they'd change.

MCAFs HL/SL
Fri Mar 5th
Music Coursework Authentication Forms (6/MCAF) are completed and signed off for every submitted component — teacher confirmation that submitted work is authentically the student's own, including verification of individual contributions in any ensemble or accompanied performances. Administrative, but non-negotiable: IB won't accept a submission without its authentication paperwork in place.
